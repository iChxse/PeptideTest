Our Story

Named for the tide that reveals what's beneath.

A king tide is the highest tide of the year — the moment the ocean pulls back the furthest and shows you the seafloor most people never see. That's the standard we're building US Kingtides around: transparency all the way down, from raw material sourcing to the certificate of analysis in every shipment.

We're a small team of formulators, chemists, and logistics people building a peptide supply company for the research community — one that treats documentation and purity as the product, not an afterthought.

Quality Process

How every batch gets verified

A repeatable four-step process stands between raw synthesis and anything that reaches a customer's bench.

01

Sourcing

Raw materials sourced from vetted, GMP-aligned synthesis partners.

02

Independent Testing

Every batch sent to an accredited third-party lab for HPLC and mass spec analysis.

03

Documentation

A batch-specific Certificate of Analysis is generated and archived.

04

Cold-Chain Fulfillment

Temperature-controlled packaging and tracked shipping on every order.
